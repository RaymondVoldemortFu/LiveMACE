- A consistent trading intent (e.g., long vs short bias, risk posture, time horizon)
- A stable decision-making focus (e.g., capital preservation vs aggressive return)
- Coherent assumptions about the market context

- Whether agents raise concerns, critiques, or alternative viewpoints that imply misalignment.
- Whether these conflicts are integrated, resolved, or explicitly dismissed.
